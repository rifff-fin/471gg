MEMBER 3 — OFFICER REVIEW, CASE DECISION, AND SIGNING

WHAT TO SHOW IN OFFICER DASHBOARD
Open: http://localhost:5173/officer-dashboard
1. Case queue: search/filter complaints.
2. Community signal: upvotes and comments.
3. Public conversation: officer can post a public comment.
4. Field-work evidence: officer sees Before/After photos.
5. Case decision: Start work, progress, hold, reject, resolve, close.
6. Signed resident update: officer must write a note.
7. Save decision: citizen gets notification and status changes.

DO NOT NEED TO SHOW
Active maintenance bridge / internal maintenance coordination. Focus on Case
decision, Field-work evidence, and Save decision.

SIMPLE EXPLANATION FOR COMPLAINT DECISION
"An officer reviews the case, chooses a decision, and writes a signed note.
The backend saves the officer name, role, time, action and note in the public
ledger. The complaint status changes and the citizen gets an inbox notification."

IMPORTANT FRONTEND FILES
frontend/src/pages/OfficerDashboard.jsx
  Officer case queue, comments, votes, field evidence, decision form.
frontend/src/pages/ComplaintDetail.jsx
  Officer can also make a signed decision from a complaint page.

IMPORTANT BACKEND FILES FOR COMPLAINTS
controllers/complaintController.js
  getOfficerComplaints: officer case queue.
  reviewComplaintByOfficer: approve/reject/hold/resolve and signed note.
  addComplaintComment: public officer/mayor comments.
  verifyCompletionReport: marks verified field work as resolved.
models/Complaint.js
  officerNotes, assignedOfficer, publicLedger, status, beforeAfterReports.
routes/complaintRoutes.js
  GET  /api/complaints/officer/cases
  POST /api/complaints/:id/review

EXACT GOVERNMENT SERVICE REQUEST FEATURE (BACKEND)
models/ServiceRequest.js
  status, officerComment, reviewedBy, reviewedAt, electronicSignature.
controllers/serviceRequestController.js
  Officer chooses Processing, Approved, Rejected, or Completed. Officer note is
  required. Electronic signature is officer name + Municipal Officer + time.
routes/serviceRequestRoutes.js
  GET   /api/service-requests/officer
  PATCH /api/service-requests/:id
